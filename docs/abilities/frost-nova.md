Frost Nova freezes the ground around Fenrir: after a short charge, every enemy within 6 m takes Frost damage.

## Effect

1. A frost ring charges around you. You can keep moving.
2. After 1.25 s, the nova bursts where you stand.
3. Every enemy within 6 m takes *Frost*{.dmg .frost} damage.

## Damage

10 base + 1.4 [per level](../reference/glossary.md#per-level).

| | Lv 0 | Lv 50 | Lv 100 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Damage *Frost*{.dmg .frost} | **10** | **80** | **150** |

## Infused

- The nova also staggers every enemy it hits. Bosses are not staggered.
