# Stagger

Stagger is a spear special attack used while blocking: you spin around, hitting nearby enemies with your spear and
staggering everything around you. Eir and Freyja share the same version.

## Effect

1. Hold block with a spear equipped and use the special attack.
2. You spin around with your spear. The spin costs no stamina.
3. The spin hits enemies with **70%** of your spear's damage.
4. Half a second later, every enemy within 4 m is staggered.

- Bosses are not staggered.
- Eir and Freyja both use the brown theme, so the ability looks and works the same for both.

## Infused

- The stagger wave also deals *Blunt*{.dmg .blunt} damage equal to **50%** of your equipped weapon's damage to every
  enemy within 4 m.
