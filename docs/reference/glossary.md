# Glossary

Definitions of terms used throughout the Norse Demigods wiki and in the mod's ability and attribute descriptions.

<!-- IMAGE: item tooltip showing an armor piece's movement penalty -->

## Heavy armor

An armor piece (helmet, chest or legs) with a movement penalty of 5% or more.

- Some attributes only work when you wear no heavy armor.
- Others need several heavy pieces; each attribute says how many.

## Per level

"Per level" always means per **demigod skill level** (0 to 100).

- Example: "1.4 damage per level" adds 70 damage at level 50 and 140 at level 100, on top of the base damage.
- Level tables show values at Lv 0, Lv 50 and Lv 100.

## Specialized weapons

The weapon types an attribute's extra effects apply to.

- Example: [Static Body](../attributes/static-body.md) spreads Lightning damage to nearby enemies only when you
  hit with axes or clubs.

## Tool tier

Determines which trees, rocks and ores an ability or attribute can damage. The mod uses the same tier scale as
vanilla axes and pickaxes.

- An ability with tool tier 2 damages everything a tier 2 tool can, and all lower tiers.
- Axes and pickaxes of the same material have different tiers (a Bronze axe is tier 2, a Bronze pickaxe tier 1).
- [Infused Earthbreaker](../items/permanent-consumables.md#infused-earthbreaker) raises the tool tier of some
  abilities and attributes.

| Tier | Same as | New trees | New rocks and ores |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Stone axe, Antler pickaxe | Beech, pine, fir, Ashlands trees | Most rocks, copper, tin |
| 1 | Flint axe, Bronze pickaxe | — | Muddy scrap piles |
| 2 | Bronze axe, Iron pickaxe | Birch, oak, ancient (swamp) trees | Silver, obsidian, glacier ice |
| 3 | Iron axe, Black metal pickaxe | — | Giant remains (black marble), meteorites, lava Leviathans (Ashlands) |
| 4 | Black metal axe, Jotun Bane | Yggdrasil shoots | — |
| 5 | — | — | Gold veins, Ashlands fortress walls and gates |
| 6 | Berserkir axes | — | Frozen troll remains |

These are vanilla Valheim values, not mod settings.

## True damage

Damage without a type, such as the core damage of Hel's [Acid Bolt](../abilities/acid-bolt.md).

- Armor, resistances and weaknesses do not change it.
- Other sources: [Decay Aura](../abilities/decay-aura.md) and the eitr burst of
  [Bloody Archer](../attributes/bloody-archer.md).

These are vanilla Valheim rules, not mod settings.
