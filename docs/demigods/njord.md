# Njord

Njord is a sea demigod who strikes with lightning bolts and tornadoes, hurls water spheres and summons necks to gather
resources. He swims and sails faster, favors axes, swords and polearms, and is built to fight in heavy armor.

!!! note
    This is the default loadout. Servers can change which abilities and attributes each demigod has.

!!! quote "Lore"
    Njord, god of the seas, is called upon for favorable winds and good luck in sailing. As the god of wealth, he is
    so rich that he can grant wealth to those who pray to him for it.

## Demigod skill experience

Njord gains demigod skill experience from dealing damage, with a bonus for Frost and Lightning damage.

| Source | Experience gained |
| --- | --- |
| Damage dealt (any source, e.g. weapons) | 0.75 per 100 damage |
| *Frost*{.dmg .frost} damage dealt | 0.5 per 100 Frost damage (on top of the damage dealt row) |
| *Lightning*{.dmg .lightning} damage dealt | 0.5 per 100 Lightning damage (on top of the damage dealt row) |

## Infusion

Infusing Njord's abilities upgrades all of them at once:

- [Lightning Bolt](../abilities/lightning-bolt.md#infused): cheaper.
- [Crush](../abilities/crush.md#infused): shorter cooldown, a Lightning explosion on landing.
- [Water Sphere](../abilities/water-sphere.md#infused): 6 spheres instead of 4.
- [Tornado](../abilities/tornado.md#infused): can stagger enemies.
- [Neck](../abilities/neck.md#infused): two necks that can chop every tree.
- [Restoration](../abilities/restoration.md#infused): removes Poison, harder to interrupt.

!!! tip "In-game hint"
    *"You can unlock the true potential of your abilities by crafting and consuming an infused material at a highly
    equipped forge. You will need crystal, and above all, the head of the queen of the seas who hunts in the night."*

??? spoiler "Infusion recipe (spoiler)"

    Craft an **Infused Crystal** at a Forge (level 7+) and consume it.

    - Only Njord can consume it.
    - It can be consumed only once.

    | Material | Amount |
    | --- | --- |
    | Thunderstone | 40 |
    | Feathers | 60 |
    | Crystal | 80 |
    | Serpent trophy | 1 |
