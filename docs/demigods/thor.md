# Thor

Thor is a lightning demigod who dashes at the speed of light and strikes enemies with lightning. He favors clubs,
axes and Himminafl, and is built to fight in heavy armor.

!!! quote "Lore"
    Thor is the god of thunder, lightning, storms, wrestling and strength, and the champion of the Aesir.
    As head of a family of strong warriors, he defends Asgard and Midgard through great feats of arms in slaying
    giants.

## Demigod skill experience

Thor gains demigod skill experience from dealing and taking damage.

| Source | Experience gained |
| --- | --- |
| Damage dealt with abilities | 1.5 per 100 damage (on top of the row below) |
| Damage dealt (any source, e.g. weapons) | 0.75 per 100 damage |
| Damage taken | 2 per 100 damage |

## Infusion

Infusing Thor's abilities upgrades all of them at once:

- [Lightning Strike](../abilities/lightning-strike.md#infused): cheaper, can chain to a second enemy.
- [Dash](../abilities/dash.md#infused): a free second dash.
- [Lightning Aura](../abilities/lightning-aura.md#infused): an electric shield against Blunt and Lightning.
- [Lightning Storm](../abilities/lightning-storm.md#infused): strikes can cause Fire explosions.
- [Restoration](../abilities/restoration.md#infused): removes Poison, harder to interrupt.

!!! tip "In-game hint"
    *"You can unlock the true potential of your abilities by crafting and consuming an infused material at a
    highly equipped forge. Can Iron Sledge be more than just a hammer?"*

??? spoiler "Infusion recipe (spoiler)"

    Craft an **Infused Hammer** at a Forge (level 7+) and consume it.

    - Only Thor can consume it.
    - It can be consumed only once.

    | Material | Amount |
    | --- | --- |
    | Thunderstone | 40 |
    | Hard antler | 15 |
    | Iron sledge | 1 |
