# Brokkr

Brokkr is a forging demigod who throws bombs that destroy entire ore veins, launches lightning torpedoes and summons a
stone golem. He favors axes, clubs and crossbows, and is built to fight in heavy armor.

## Demigod skill experience

Brokkr gains demigod skill experience from dealing damage.

| Source | Experience gained |
| --- | --- |
| Damage dealt (any source, e.g. weapons) | 1 per 100 damage |

## Infusion

Infusing Brokkr's abilities upgrades all of them at once:

- [Lightning Torpedo](../abilities/lightning-torpedo.md#infused): cheaper, turns faster, targets the closest enemy.
- [Combustion](../abilities/combustion.md#infused): shorter cooldown, stronger forward launch.
- [Bomb](../abilities/bomb.md#infused): shorter cooldown, breaks harder ores.
- [Stone Shield](../abilities/stone-shield.md#infused): lasts longer.
- [Stone Golem](../abilities/stone-golem.md#infused): shorter cooldown.
- [Nano Cube](../abilities/nano-cube.md#infused): larger cube.

!!! tip "In-game hint"
    *"You can unlock the true potential of your abilities by crafting and consuming an infused material at a
    highly equipped forge. You are so skilled that nothing is needed other than iron and Odin's thunderstones."*

??? spoiler "Infusion recipe (spoiler)"

    Craft an **Infused Iron** at a Forge (level 7+) and consume it.

    - Only Brokkr can consume it.
    - It can be consumed only once.
    - With [Blacksmith](../attributes/blacksmith.md), a level 6 Forge counts as level 7.

    | Material | Amount |
    | --- | --- |
    | Thunderstone | 40 |
    | Iron | 80 |
