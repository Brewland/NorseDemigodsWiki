# Hel

Hel is a demigod of the underworld who drains the life of her enemies, calls down meteors and raises skeletons to fight
for her. She favors two-handed axes and swords, polearms and knives, and fights in heavy or light armor.

!!! note
    This is the default loadout. Servers can change which abilities and attributes each demigod has.

!!! quote "Lore"
    Hel, daughter of the trickster god Loki and sister of Fenrir, is the goddess of the underworld where many of the
    dead dwell. Greedy, harsh, and cruel. Her kingdom is said to lie downward and northward. It is called Niflheim,
    or the World of Darkness.

## Demigod skill experience

Hel gains demigod skill experience from her own damage and from her minions' damage.

| Source | Experience gained |
| --- | --- |
| Damage dealt (any source, e.g. weapons) | 0.75 per 100 damage |
| Damage dealt by your minions (skeletons and ghost) | 1.5 per 100 damage |

## Infusion

Infusing Hel's abilities upgrades all of them at once:

- [Acid Bolt](../abilities/acid-bolt.md#infused): cheaper.
- [Meteor](../abilities/meteor.md#infused): cheaper.
- [Decay Aura](../abilities/decay-aura.md#infused): also restores stamina and eitr.
- [Minions](../abilities/minions.md#infused): a ghost joins your three skeletons.
- [Sacrifice](../abilities/sacrifice.md#infused): sacrificed skeletons explode; restores eitr without minions.

!!! tip "In-game hint"
    *"You can unlock the true potential of your abilities by crafting and consuming an infused material at a highly
    equipped forge. You walk in the shadows along with wraiths; these creatures fly over the swamp at night. Nothing
    is infused without Odin's thunderstones."*

??? spoiler "Infusion recipe (spoiler)"

    Craft an **Infused Bones** at a Forge (level 7+) and consume it.

    - Only Hel can consume it.
    - It can be consumed only once.

    | Material | Amount |
    | --- | --- |
    | Thunderstone | 40 |
    | Bone fragments | 200 |
    | Wraith trophy | 1 |
