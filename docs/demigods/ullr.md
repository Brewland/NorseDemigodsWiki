# Ullr

Ullr is a hunter demigod who fights with frost-infused arrows, a wolf companion and stealth. He favors bows, knives,
spears and axes, and fights best in light armor.

!!! note
    This is the default loadout. Servers can change which abilities and attributes each demigod has.

!!! quote "Lore"
    Ullr is the Norse god of winter, hunting and archery. He also ruled over Asgard, home of the most powerful Norse
    gods, when Odin was away. Many would pray to Ullr before undertaking travel in the harsh winter.

## Demigod skill experience

Ullr gains demigod skill experience from dealing damage, with a bonus for bows, crossbows and sneak attacks.

| Source | Experience gained |
| --- | --- |
| Damage dealt (any source, e.g. weapons) | 0.75 per 100 damage |
| Damage dealt with bows or crossbows | 0.5 per 100 damage (on top of the damage dealt row) |
| Sneak attack damage | 2.25 per 100 damage (on top of the damage dealt row) |

## Infusion

Infusing Ullr's abilities upgrades all of them at once:

- [Infused Arrow](../abilities/infused-arrow.md#infused): a bigger Blunt and Frost explosion where the arrow lands.
- [Blast](../abilities/blast.md#infused): twice the push, staggers enemies.
- [Sense Creatures](../abilities/sense-creatures.md#infused): also shows every other hostile creature.
- [Summon Wolf](../abilities/summon-wolf.md#infused): your wolf reflects Frost damage at attackers.
- [Heal Over Time](../abilities/heal-over-time.md#infused): removes Burning.

!!! tip "In-game hint"
    *"You can unlock the true potential of your abilities by crafting and consuming an infused material at a highly
    equipped forge. Hunt the kin of the supreme deer. Nothing is infused without Odin's thunderstones."*

??? spoiler "Infusion recipe (spoiler)"

    Craft **Infused Feathers** at a Forge (level 7+) and consume it.

    - Only Ullr can consume it.
    - It can be consumed only once.

    | Material | Amount |
    | --- | --- |
    | Thunderstone | 40 |
    | Feathers | 200 |
    | Eikthyr trophy | 1 |
