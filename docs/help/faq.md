# FAQ

Short answers to the questions players ask most often about Norse Demigods. Each answer links to the page with the
full details.

<!-- IMAGE: the in-game compendium open on ND: Introduction -->

## Getting started

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Is there a list of every demigod and ability?

Yes, this wiki: start with [Demigods](../demigods/index.md) and [Abilities](../abilities/index.md).

In the game, your compendium shows your own demigod's abilities, keys and attributes once you have chosen one. See
[The compendium](../getting-started/first-steps.md#the-compendium).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I choose a demigod?

Use the [Demigod Runestone](../items/demigod-runestone.md) from your inventory and pick a demigod from its menu.

- You get one for free when you spawn without a demigod.
- No runestone? Craft one from your inventory; it needs no crafting station. Servers can change the recipe or turn
  off the free runestone. See [Getting one](../items/demigod-runestone.md#getting-one).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### The runestone says "Disabled by divine intervention!"

The server has disabled that demigod, so it cannot be chosen. Players who already are that demigod keep it.

- Setting: `[(General)] Disabled Demigods List` (see [Optional Features](../getting-started/optional-features.md#other-features)).
- If every demigod shows the message, check this setting first.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can I switch to another demigod?

Yes, with the [Demigod Runestone](../items/demigod-runestone.md). You lose a few demigod skill levels and your ability
infusion, but keep your permanent consumables. See
[Switching demigods](../items/demigod-runestone.md#switching-demigods).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can I keep my infusion when I switch demigods?

No. Infusion belongs to one demigod, and you must infuse again after switching, even when you switch back.

Server admins can infuse a player with the `InfuseAbilities` [console command](console-commands.md).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Does the mod work in multiplayer?

Yes. The server and every player need the same mod version and the same data files. See
[Multiplayer](../getting-started/installing-the-mod.md#multiplayer).

</div>

## Controls

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I use my abilities?

Abilities use your side mouse buttons, block plus a side mouse button, and the special attack key while you hold one
of your demigod's favorite weapons. See [Controls and Hotkeys](../getting-started/controls-and-hotkeys.md).

Your compendium page **ND: Abilities** lists every key for your demigod.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### My mouse has no side buttons. How do I change the keys?

Set your own keys in a configuration manager in-game, or in the settings file. See
[Changing keys](../getting-started/controls-and-hotkeys.md#changing-keys).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I use my weapon's normal special attack?

Hold ++left-alt++ while you use the special attack. See
[Special attacks](../getting-started/controls-and-hotkeys.md#special-attacks).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I use a bow's special attack?

With a bow or crossbow equipped, press your special attack key (++mouse-middle++ by default), as with any other
weapon. Only demigods with a bow special attack, such as Ullr's [Infused Arrow](../abilities/infused-arrow.md), have
one.

</div>

## Progression and infusion

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I gain demigod experience?

Each demigod gains experience in its own way, mostly by fighting. Each demigod page lists its sources under
**Demigod skill experience**. See [Progression](../getting-started/progression.md).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### My demigod level stopped rising. Why?

Your demigod skill is capped until you have the next boss's trophy.

- Killing the boss is not enough: you must pick up its trophy (head), so it appears in your trophy list.
- In multiplayer, each player needs the trophy in their own list: pass the head around.

See [Skill cap](../getting-started/progression.md#skill-cap).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I infuse my abilities?

Each demigod has its own infusion item. Consume it once to upgrade all of your demigod's abilities.

- Your compendium page **ND: Introduction** gives a hint in its last paragraph.
- The recipes are on [Infusion Items](../items/infusion-items.md) and in each demigod's **Infusion** section,
  hidden in spoiler boxes.
- Each ability page lists what infusion adds in its **Infused** section.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I infuse Kvasir?

Kvasir has no infusion item: he must defeat a specific enemy.

- Your compendium page **ND: Introduction** gives a hint.
- The exact condition, including what counts in multiplayer, is in the spoiler box on the
  [Kvasir](../demigods/kvasir.md#infusion) page.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Where can I find the recipes?

On the [Items](../items/index.md) pages. Recipes for infusion items, permanent consumables and the Infused
Megingjord are hidden in spoiler boxes; food and runestone recipes are shown openly.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Do vanilla skills such as Elemental Magic or Blood Magic make my abilities stronger?

No. Ability damage grows with your demigod skill level. Each ability page shows it in a level table.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Do my companions get stronger as I level up?

Yes. Their damage and health grow with your demigod skill level. A level gained counts from your next summon, except
for your wolf, which grows stronger right away. See
[How companions work](../companions/index.md#how-companions-work).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Is there special gear for demigods?

- [Infused Megingjord](../items/infused-megingjord.md): a belt for any demigod.
- [Permanent Consumables](../items/permanent-consumables.md): items any demigod can consume for permanent bonuses.
- [Sweet Fish Foods](../items/sweet-fish-foods.md): strong foods that anyone can eat.
- Fenrir gets extra bonuses from two vanilla armor sets. See [Fenrir › Class quirks](../demigods/fenrir.md#class-quirks).

</div>

## Abilities and combat

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Why can't my abilities damage harder trees or ores?

Every ability that damages trees, rocks or ores has a [tool tier](../reference/glossary.md#tool-tier). Harder
materials need a higher tier.

- Infusion raises the tier of some abilities. Each ability page lists it.
- [Infused Earthbreaker](../items/permanent-consumables.md#infused-earthbreaker) and
  [Infused Grand Earthbreaker](../items/permanent-consumables.md#infused-grand-earthbreaker) raise it further.
- Fenrir's claws use his Unarmed skill instead. See [Titan Arms](../attributes/titan-arms.md#felling-trees).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Why do my abilities sometimes stop damaging trees and rocks?

That is collateral protection: trees and ores are spared while an enemy is close to you or to the ability's area. It
stops you from wrecking the landscape in a fight.

- Setting: `[(General)] Protect Resources From Collateral Damage` (on by default; outdoors only).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can I stop abilities from damaging the world?

- **Buildings:** with ward protection on, players cannot damage buildings inside an active player ward. See
  [Optional Features](../getting-started/optional-features.md#other-features).
- **Trees and ores:** server admins can set an ability's `Damage To Resources` settings to 0 in its config section.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### What counts as heavy armor?

Armor pieces that slow you down. Modded armor follows the same rule. See
[Heavy armor](../reference/glossary.md#heavy-armor).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Does a mod that removes armor movement penalties work with heavy-armor attributes?

It depends on how that mod works. The mod checks each armor piece's own movement penalty.

- If the other mod removes the penalty from the armor itself, the armor counts as light.
- If it only cancels the slowdown and the armor keeps its penalty, the armor still counts as heavy.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can Dash or Warp take me upward?

No. [Dash](../abilities/dash.md) and [Warp](../abilities/warp.md) move you forward in the direction you face, and the
landing spot can be only a little higher or lower than you. Thor's Dash can also dive down to a spot you aim at while
airborne.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can Fenrir use weapons and tools?

Fenrir fights with his bare claws and cannot equip melee weapons or shields. He can use fist weapons, bows,
crossbows, staffs and pickaxes, and his claws fell trees. See [Titan Arms](../attributes/titan-arms.md#weapons).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can I keep my weapon in hand when Brokkr throws a bomb?

Server admins can set `[Ability Bomb] Hide Weapons` = `false`. See [Bomb](../abilities/bomb.md).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can I turn off visual effects or sounds?

These are your own settings, so you can change them on any server:

| What | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Passive effects, such as Thor's lightning or Surtr's fire | `[(General)] Enable Passive Special Effects` |
| Water Shield's splash and sound near fire | `[Attribute Water Shield] Play Fire Proximity Effects` |
| The ghostly look of Ullr's wolf | `[Ability Wolf] Apply Ghost Effect` |

Hel's skeletons shake the screen like skeletons raised with the vanilla Dead Raiser. Lower the camera shake in the
game's settings.

</div>

## Companions

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I summon or dismiss a companion?

Use its ability (see its page for the key).

- Wolves and necks stay until you use the ability again to dismiss them, or until they die.
- Other companions last a set time or until killed.
- All companions disappear when you teleport, die or log out.

See the [Companions](../companions/index.md#overview) overview.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can I summon companions in dungeons?

Not most of them: Summon Wolf, Stone Golem, Summon Surtling and Neck cannot be used indoors, such as in dungeons and
caves. Hel's [Minions](../abilities/minions.md) and Kvasir's [Charm](../abilities/charm.md) work indoors. Each ability
page lists its limits.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I feed my wolf?

Feed it like a tamed wolf: drop food it eats on the ground near it. While it is fed, it deals more damage. See
[Feeding](../abilities/summon-wolf.md#feeding).

Other companions never need food.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Does my wolf keep its stars?

Yes. It gains stars by hitting enemies and keeps them between summons, also when you teleport, die or log out. It
loses them only when it dies. See [Stars](../abilities/summon-wolf.md#stars).

</div>

## Servers and settings

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Where are the mod's settings?

In `BepInEx/config/NorseDemigods.cfg`. The easiest way to change them is in-game, with a configuration manager. See
[Settings](../getting-started/installing-the-mod.md#settings) and
[Recommended Mods](../getting-started/recommended-mods.md#configurationmanager).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Which settings does the server control?

Most of them: the server sends its settings to players when they join, and only admins can change them. Your own
settings are the ones about you and your screen: hotkeys, the interface and visual effects. See
[Settings](../getting-started/installing-the-mod.md#settings).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Do I need to restart the game after changing a setting?

- **Configuration manager:** most changes apply right away. Values fixed when something starts, such as a
  companion's damage, apply from the next time.
- **Settings file:** edit it while the game is closed.
- **Always a restart:** `[(General)] Enable Mod`, and changes to `Demigods.yaml` and `Consumables.json`.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I disable a demigod or an ability on my server?

- **A demigod:** add it to `[(General)] Disabled Demigods List`. See
  [Optional Features](../getting-started/optional-features.md#other-features).
- **Abilities and attributes:** edit `Demigods.yaml`. Every player needs the same file as the server. See
  [Multiplayer](../getting-started/installing-the-mod.md#multiplayer).
- **Teleport:** set `[Teleportation] Enable Teleport Ability` = `false`.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can players be made to craft the Demigod Runestone?

Yes. Turn off the free runestone with `[Runestone] Runestone Spawn At Start`, and change the recipe with
`[Runestone] Runestone Crafting Recipe`. See [Getting one](../items/demigod-runestone.md#getting-one).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### How do I make the game harder?

Raise the mod's difficulty. See [Difficulty](../getting-started/difficulty.md). It works together with mods that
change creature levels, such as Creature Level and Loot Control.

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Are there console commands?

Yes, for example to infuse your abilities or remove your demigod. See [Console Commands](console-commands.md).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Is the mod compatible with other mods?

See [Compatibility](../getting-started/compatibility.md) for known problems and the mods the mod supports. A mod
that is not listed is not known to cause problems.

</div>

## Troubleshooting

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Some text is missing or shows as placeholders

The mod's files were moved or are missing: reinstall the mod and keep its folder structure. See
[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#common-problems).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Something isn't working. How do I report a bug?

See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

</div>

<div class="nd-faq" markdown>

### Can I translate the mod?

Yes. Translate the English text files and share them on the [Norse Demigods Discord](https://discord.gg/gSQHmE3yap)
so they can be included in the mod. See [Languages](../getting-started/installing-the-mod.md#languages) for the
languages already included.

</div>
