# Recommended Mods

Two optional mods that make Norse Demigods easier to use. Neither is required: the mod is fully playable without
them.

| [ConfigurationManager](#configurationmanager) | shudnal | Change settings and hotkeys in-game | [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/shudnal/ConfigurationManager/) · [Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/shudnal/ConfigurationManager) |

## NorseDemigodsUI

An addon made for Norse Demigods.

- An ability bar with hotkeys and cooldowns.
- Energy and companion meters.
- Detailed stats and tooltips.
- A built-in wiki for your demigod.

## ConfigurationManager

A configuration manager: a settings window for all your mods, opened in-game.

- Change the mod's settings without editing the settings file.
- Bind hotkeys by pressing the key you want (see [Controls and Hotkeys](controls-and-hotkeys.md#changing-keys)).
- Hotkey and interface changes apply right away.
