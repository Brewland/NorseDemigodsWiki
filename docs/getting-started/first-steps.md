# First Steps

What to do the first time you play with Norse Demigods: read the compendium, choose a demigod with the Demigod
Runestone and set up your keys.

<!-- IMAGE: the compendium open on "ND: Introduction" -->

## Before you start

- **Use a new character if you can.** The mod works with existing characters, but your demigod skill starts at
  level 0. Some demigods, such as [Fenrir](../demigods/fenrir.md), rely on it for most of their damage, and
  catching up late can be slow.
- **If you host the world, choose the [difficulty](difficulty.md) first.** Demigods are strong, and the default
  changes nothing.

## Your first session

1. **Spawn in.** A message tells you to look in your compendium. A free
   [Demigod Runestone](../items/demigod-runestone.md) is placed in your inventory.
2. **Open the compendium.** Open your inventory (++tab++), click the bird icon, and read **ND: Introduction**.
3. **Use the runestone.** Use it from your inventory, read about each demigod, and press *"I choose you!"*. See
   [Demigods](../demigods/index.md) to compare them first.
4. **Read the compendium again.** It now has pages about your demigod, including your ability keys.
5. **Check your keys.** Abilities A and B use the side mouse buttons by default. If your mouse has none, bind
   other keys: see [Controls and Hotkeys](controls-and-hotkeys.md).

If you lose the runestone, you get a new one the next time you spawn, as long as you are not a demigod yet. You
can also craft one from 1 Stone and 1 Resin, without a crafting station.

## The compendium

After you choose a demigod, these pages appear in the compendium. They always match your server's settings.

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| ND: Introduction | The basics, then a hint about your demigod's infusion (last paragraph) |
| ND: Abilities | Your abilities, with their keys and key combinations |
| ND: Standard Attributes | Your demigod's attributes |
| ND: Acquired Attributes | The permanent effects you have gained, such as [permanent consumables](../items/permanent-consumables.md) |
| ND: Status | Your current demigod bonuses (and your wolf's, if it is out) |
| ND: Wolves | Your wolf's details (Fenrir and Ullr only) |

Before you choose a demigod, only ND: Introduction is there.

## Changing your mind

- **Switch demigods:** use the runestone again and pick another demigod. You lose 5 demigod skill levels, but keep
  your permanent consumables, and each demigod keeps its ability infusion for when you switch back. See
  [Switching demigods](../items/demigod-runestone.md#switching-demigods).
- **Stop being a demigod:** type `RevokeDemigod` in the game console (++f5++). It needs no cheats, but the
  console only opens if the game was started with the `-console` launch option (vanilla).

!!! warning "RevokeDemigod cannot be undone"
    It removes your demigod, every ability infusion (including those remembered for other demigods) and your
    permanent consumables, and resets your demigod skill to level 0.
