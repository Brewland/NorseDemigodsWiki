# Getting Started

Everything a new player needs, in order. **Setup** happens before you launch the game: installing the mod, other
mods and server settings. **Playing** covers what to do in the game.

## Setup

  guide.
- [Recommended Mods](recommended-mods.md): NorseDemigodsUI and a configuration manager.
- [Compatibility](compatibility.md): mods known to cause problems, built-in support and bridge mods.
- [Difficulty](difficulty.md): how the mod's difficulty works and which one to choose.
- [Optional Features](optional-features.md): settings that make materials easier to get, and other features.

## Playing

- [How the Mod Works](how-the-mod-works.md): demigods, abilities, attributes, energy, the demigod skill and more.
- [Controls and Hotkeys](controls-and-hotkeys.md): default keys, special attacks and how to change them.
- [First Steps](first-steps.md): the compendium, the Demigod Runestone and your first session.
