# Thirsty

Thirsty is Kvasir's own attribute: every enemy he kills restores some of his health and stamina, and may give him
energy.

</div>

## Effects

Each time you kill an enemy:

| Effect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Health restored | **3%** of your maximum health |
| Stamina restored | 5% of your maximum stamina |
| Chance to regenerate 1 energy | 30% |

- It counts as your kill when your hit was the last one it took, with any weapon or ability.
- Tamed creatures and companions do not count.
