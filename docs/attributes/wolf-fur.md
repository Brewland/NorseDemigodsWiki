# Wolf Fur

Wolf Fur is Ullr's own attribute: without heavy armor, his fur gives him extra armor and absorbs part of all damage.
He is resistant to Frost damage.

## Scaling

Without any heavy armor piece: 0.2 extra armor and 0.2% less damage taken
[per level](../reference/glossary.md#per-level).

| | Lv 0 | Lv 50 | Lv 100 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Extra armor | 0 | 10 | 20 |
| Damage taken reduction | 0% | 10% | 20% |

- The damage reduction applies to all damage types.
- The armor is rounded down to a whole number.

## Resistances

- *Frost*{.dmg .frost} Resistant (unless you are already very resistant or immune)
