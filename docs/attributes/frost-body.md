# Frost Body

Frost Body is Fenrir's own attribute: the cold air around his body gives him extra armor when he wears no heavy armor,
adds Frost damage to his arrows and frost staffs, and makes him very resistant to Frost.

## Weapon damage

| Weapon | Extra damage *Frost*{.dmg .frost} |
| --- | ---: |
| Bows and crossbows | +10% |
| Frost staffs (at least 75% Frost damage, such as the Staff of Frost) | +70% |

- The staff bonus does not apply to the [Ice Shards](../abilities/ice-shards.md) ability.

## Defense

Extra armor when wearing no heavy armor: 0.4 [per level](../getting-started/glossary.md#per-level).

| | Lv 0 | Lv 50 | Lv 100 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Extra armor | 0 | 20 | 40 |

| Effect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| *Frost*{.dmg .frost} resistance | Very resistant |
| Base eitr | +25 |
