# Holy Body

Holy Body is Eir's own attribute: the light shining on her body adds Spirit damage to her weapons, spreads it to nearby
enemies, sometimes as an explosion, and gives her extra health and eitr.

## Weapon damage

Extra damage added to your weapon hits:

| Weapon | Extra damage *Spirit*{.dmg .spirit} |
| --- | ---: |
| Bows and crossbows | +20% |
| Spears and swords | +20% |
| Axes, clubs and polearms | +10% |

- Knives and unarmed attacks get no extra damage.
- **Spirit splash:** hits with spears or swords also deal **50%** of the hit's damage as *Spirit*{.dmg .spirit} to
  other enemies within 2 m.
- **Explosion:** each splash has a 40% chance to become an explosion that hits enemies within 5 m instead. The
  explosion needs at least two enemies within 5 m; otherwise that hit spreads no damage.
