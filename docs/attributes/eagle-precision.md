# Eagle Precision

Eagle Precision is Hraesvelgr's own attribute: his sword and polearm hits spread Pierce damage to nearby enemies, his
other weapons and bows deal extra Pierce damage, and his arrows fly faster and straighter.

## Weapon damage

| Weapon | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Swords and polearms | **Pierce splash:** **25%** of the hit's damage as *Pierce*{.dmg .pierce} to other enemies within 2 m of the target |
| Axes, clubs, knives and spears | +10% extra damage as *Pierce*{.dmg .pierce} |
| Bows and crossbows | +10% extra damage as *Pierce*{.dmg .pierce} |

- Unarmed attacks and claws get no extra damage.
- The splash only comes from your weapon's own hits, not from abilities such as [Cleave](../abilities/cleave.md).

## Arrows

With a bow (not a crossbow):

| Effect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow accuracy | +20% |
| Arrow speed | +25% |
