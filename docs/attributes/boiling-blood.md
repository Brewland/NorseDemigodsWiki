# Boiling Blood

Boiling Blood is Fenrir's own attribute: when he sits down out of combat, he becomes Rested anywhere, even in the cold,
and quickly regenerates health. In exchange, he is weak to Poison.

## Effects

While sitting and out of combat:

| Effect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Resting status | Applied, without needing a fire or shelter |
| Health regeneration | 4% of your maximum health per second |

- Out of combat means no enemy has noticed you, and you are not burning, poisoned, frozen, shocked or freezing.
- Standing up stops the regeneration.

## Weakness

- *Poison*{.dmg .poison} Weak (unless something else already changes your Poison resistance).
