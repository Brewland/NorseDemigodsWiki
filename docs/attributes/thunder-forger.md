# Thunder Forger

Thunder Forger is Brokkr's own attribute: the static energy of his forged weapons adds Lightning damage to his melee
hits, spreads it to nearby enemies, and makes him faster with two-handed weapons and crossbows.

## Weapon damage

Extra damage added to your melee hits:

| Weapon | Extra damage *Lightning*{.dmg .lightning} |
| --- | ---: |
| Axes and clubs | +10% |
| Polearms, spears and swords | +5% |

- Knives, unarmed attacks, bows and crossbows get no extra damage.
- **Lightning splash:** hits with axes or clubs also deal **35%** of the hit's damage as
  *Lightning*{.dmg .lightning} to other enemies within 2 m.
