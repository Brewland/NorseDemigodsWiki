# Child of Darkness

Child of Darkness is Hel's own attribute: at night she is Rested anywhere outdoors, without a fire or shelter. In
exchange, she is weak to Spirit.

## Effects

At night, outdoors:

- If you are not Rested, you become Rested, without needing a fire, bed or shelter.
- While the night lasts, your remaining Rested time grows by 2 s every second, up to its full duration.
- The full duration follows the vanilla Rested rules (longer with more comfort).

## Weakness

- *Spirit*{.dmg .spirit} Weak (unless something else already changes your Spirit resistance).
