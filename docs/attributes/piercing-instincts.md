# Piercing Instincts

Piercing Instincts is Kvasir's own attribute: his knives and claws deal extra Pierce damage, and his knives can chop
trees and mine ores.

## Weapon damage

| Weapon | Extra damage *Pierce*{.dmg .pierce} |
| --- | ---: |
| Knives | +20% |
| Claws (fist weapons) | +15% |

- Bare hands get no extra damage.

## Knives as tools

Your knives also deal *Chop*{.dmg .chop} and *Pickaxe*{.dmg .pickaxe} damage, so they can fell trees and mine rocks
and ores:

- Chop and pickaxe damage: **175%** each of the knife's Pierce damage, counting the +20% bonus above.
- [Tool tier](../getting-started/glossary.md#tool-tier) 1.
- Knife hits on trees raise your Woodcutting skill.
