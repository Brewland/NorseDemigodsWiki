# Glide

Glide is Hraesvelgr's own attribute: he always falls slowly, gliding through the air, and takes no fall damage.

## Effects

- You always have the vanilla slow fall effect, the same one the Feather cape gives: you fall slowly and take no fall
  damage when you land.
- Wind effects appear around you after you have been falling for 1.5 s.

## Notes

- [Crush](../abilities/crush.md) cancels slow falling, but Hraesvelgr does not have Crush by default.
- Hraesvelgr gains demigod skill experience for every second he is airborne (see
  [Hraesvelgr](../demigods/hraesvelgr.md#demigod-skill-experience)).
