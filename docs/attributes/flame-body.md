# Flame Body

Flame Body is Surtr's own attribute: the flames around his body add Fire damage to his weapons and spread it to nearby
enemies, give him extra eitr and energy, and make him very resistant to Fire and immune to heat.

## Weapon damage

Extra damage added to your weapon hits:

| Weapon | Extra damage *Fire*{.dmg .fire} |
| --- | ---: |
| Bows and crossbows | +10% |
| Axes, swords and polearms | +10% |
| Clubs and spears | +5% |

- Knives and unarmed attacks get no extra damage.
- **Fire splash:** hits with axes, swords or polearms also deal **35%** of the hit's damage as *Fire*{.dmg .fire} to
  other enemies within 2 m. Himminafl gets no splash.

## Resources and defense

| Effect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Base eitr | +25 |
| Chance every second to regenerate 1 energy | 10% |
