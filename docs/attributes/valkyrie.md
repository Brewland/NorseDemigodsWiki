# Valkyrie

Valkyrie is the attribute of the shield maidens Eir and Freyja: it strengthens your shield, gives you energy when
you block, and makes thrown spears faster, deadlier and always return to you.

## Shields

| Effect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Chance to regenerate 1 energy when blocking an enemy's attack | 70% |
| Chance to stagger the attacker when blocking with a tower shield | 25% |
| Blocking skill experience | ×3 (300%) |

Extra block armor for shields: 5 base + 0.1 [per level](../getting-started/glossary.md#per-level).

| | Lv 0 | Lv 50 | Lv 100 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Extra shield block armor | 5 | 10 | 15 |

- The energy chance needs something in your off hand, such as a shield.
- Bosses are not staggered.

## Thrown spears

| Effect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Thrown spear damage | +70% |
| Thrown spear speed | +30% |
| Chance to regenerate 1 energy when a thrown spear hits an enemy | 70% |
| Chance to stagger an enemy hit by a thrown spear | 40% |
| Spears skill experience | ×1.5 (150%) |

- Bosses are not staggered.

## Spear return

A thrown spear always comes back to your inventory:

- after it lands,
- if it flies 100 m or more away from you,
- when you teleport or log out.

It is equipped again if your hands are free. If your inventory is full, it drops at your feet or stays where it
landed.

## Notes

- No energy regeneration while an energy-draining ability is active.
