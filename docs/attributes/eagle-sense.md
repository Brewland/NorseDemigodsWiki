# Eagle Sense

Eagle Sense is Hraesvelgr's own attribute: when he stands still outdoors, blue trails of wind appear and flow towards
his home, guiding him back without a map.

## Effect

1. Stand still (no movement input) or sit down outdoors.
2. After 5–8 s, a trail of blue leaves flows from you towards your home. A new trail follows every 2–3 s.
3. Each trail travels 50 m over 10 s, at your own height.

- "Home" is where you would respawn: your bed, or the world spawn if you have no bed.
- Moving resets the wait. It does not work indoors, such as in dungeons and caves.
