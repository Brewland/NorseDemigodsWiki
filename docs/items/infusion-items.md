# Infusion Items

Infusion items are crafted materials that awaken the true power of a demigod's abilities: consuming one upgrades
all of that demigod's abilities at once, for good. Each demigod has its own item, except Kvasir, whose infusion
comes from a kill.

## How infusion works

- Only the demigod named on the item can consume it, and only once.
- Infusion upgrades every ability of that demigod. Each ability page lists what it adds in its **Infused** section.
- Switching to another demigod with the [Demigod Runestone](demigod-runestone.md) removes the infusion. This also
  happens if you later switch back: you must infuse again.
- Each demigod's introduction in the in-game compendium gives a hint about its infusion.
- Recipes and crafting stations are deliberately hidden in the game, so they are in spoiler boxes below.

## Kvasir

Kvasir has no infusion item: his infusion comes from a kill. What it upgrades:
[Kvasir › Infusion](../demigods/kvasir.md#infusion).

??? spoiler "Infusion condition (spoiler)"

    Kill **Brenna**, the fire skeleton that guards one of Hildir's chests (vanilla).

    - Kvasir must land the killing blow, or be within 50 m of Brenna when it dies.
    - It happens only once. In-game message when it does: *"You feel more powerful"*.
    - The in-game demigod overview also shows roughly how far Brenna's tomb is from the world spawn, and in which
      direction.
