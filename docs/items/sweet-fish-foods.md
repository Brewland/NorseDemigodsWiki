# Sweet Fish Foods

Sweet fish foods are dishes made from raw fish and honey that give health, stamina and eitr at once, which makes
them a strong food for demigods. Anyone can eat them, but only one sweet fish food can be active at a time.

## How they work

- They are food: eat them like any vanilla food. Anyone can eat them, demigod or not.
- **One at a time:** while a sweet fish food is active, you cannot eat a different one. You can still eat the same
  one again, as with vanilla food.
- They stack up to 10 per inventory slot.

## Getting the ingredients

- **Raw fish:** with this mod, the vanilla recipe that turns a Perch into raw fish needs no crafting station.
- **Fish:** you can also catch fish by throwing an [Abyssal Harpoon](abyssal-harpoon.md) at them.
- **Honey:** from beehives (vanilla). Server settings in `[Honey]`:

| Setting | Default | Config |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum honey per beehive | 4 (vanilla) | `[Honey] Max Honey Produced By Beehive` |
| **If enabled:** bees need no free space around the beehive | Off | `[Honey] Ignore Beehive Proximity Check` |
| **If enabled:** chance of a queen bee per honey taken | 0% | `[Honey] Queen Bee Spawn Chance (%)` |
| **If enabled:** Haldor sells queen bees | 150 coins | `[Merchant] Enable Custom Merchant Items`, `[Merchant] Queen Bee Price` |
